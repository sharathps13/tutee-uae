    Frequently asked

    What are the basic requirements to study in the UAE?

    You need a valid passport, strong academic transcripts and proof of English proficiency. The CAA sets the national minimum: EmSAT Achieve English 1100, TOEFL 500 (61 iBT) or IELTS Academic 5.0 for an undergraduate program, and EmSAT 1400, TOEFL 550 (79 iBT) or IELTS 6.0 for a graduate one. Your certificates must also be attested by the issuing country and the UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs, which is the step students most often under-plan. Some graduate programs — MBAs in particular — may also ask for a GMAT score or work experience.

    How does the UAE Student Residence Visa process work?

    You do not apply for it yourself — your institution does. Once you accept an offer from a CAA-licensed institution, it opens your visa file and applies to ICP, or to GDRFA in Dubai, for an entry permit. You travel on that permit, then complete a medical fitness test (blood test and chest X-ray), register for your Emirates ID and take out the health insurance every UAE resident must hold. The residence visa is then issued for one year and renewed annually on a continuation-of-study letter. There is no credibility interview and no published maintenance-funds threshold. We provide comprehensive guidance for every step.

    Can I get financial aid or an education loan?

    Yes! We have exclusive tie-ups with top financial institutions and banks to provide both collateral and non-collateral education loans. These loans can cover your tuition fees, accommodation and living expenses with competitive interest rates.

    Can my family relocate with me to the UAE?

    Yes, and the UAE is more generous here than most destinations. A 2021 Cabinet resolution lets students at UAE universities sponsor family members at any study level, so this does not depend on your course. The real condition is housing: you must show suitable accommodation for them, which in Dubai means a tenancy contract registered with Ejari, and every relative over 18 must pass a medical fitness test. Their visas run one year and renew alongside yours. A dependant who wants to work needs their own work permit.

    Can I work while I study in the UAE?

    Not automatically. A UAE student visa carries no automatic right to work: you need a No Objection Certificate from your institution, and your employer must obtain a work permit from MoHRE or from the free zone the job sits in. University students are named on MoHRE’s Private Teacher Work Permit, which is free and valid two years. No official weekly hour limit is published, so be sceptical of sites that quote one.

    What can I do after I graduate in the UAE?

    The main route is the Green Residence: five years, self-sponsored, with no employer needed, which you apply for within a year of graduating in order to look for work or start a business. Outstanding graduates can be nominated for the Golden Residence, a renewable ten-year permit that also lets you sponsor your family. Be clear on one point, because it differs from most study destinations: UAE residence is always renewable rather than permanent, and there is no settlement or citizenship route at the end of it.

    Do you help with accommodation and pre-departure?

    Yes, we partner with top student housing providers to secure safe and comfortable living spaces near your campus. We also conduct comprehensive pre-departure briefings covering UAE culture and law, academic expectations, packing lists and immigration procedures.
